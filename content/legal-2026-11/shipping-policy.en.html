All KORNNE products are manufactured and shipped by Mezbaha Teknolojileri from our factory in Dilovası, Kocaeli, Türkiye.

Orders within Türkiye

Orders placed on the Turkish-language site are priced in Turkish lira including VAT. No separate shipping charge is added to the order total. Goods are delivered to the delivery address given at checkout by our contracted carrier or, for large equipment, by freight transport. Delivery takes place within 30 days of the order confirmation at the latest; for made-to-order equipment the lead time stated on the product page or in the order confirmation applies.

Shipping cost outside Türkiye

Every order shipped outside Türkiye is charged a flat shipping fee of USD 100 per order, regardless of the number of items. The fee is shown in the cart and at checkout before you place your order and is included in your order total. No other shipping charge is added after checkout.

Customs duties and import taxes

Prices and the shipping fee for orders outside Türkiye do not include import duties, customs clearance fees or local taxes (such as US sales tax or VAT) of the destination country. These are charged by the authorities or the carrier in your country and are paid by the buyer on delivery.

Processing and delivery time

We confirm the dispatch date in writing together with your order confirmation. Orders are processed once payment has been received (card payment approved or bank transfer credited to our account). Made-to-order equipment is prepared after payment is received. International transit time depends on the destination and the carrier, and customs clearance time is not included; we share the tracking details once the goods are dispatched.

Your own freight forwarder

If you prefer to use your own freight forwarder, tell us when you place the order. We prepare the export packaging and commercial documents (commercial invoice, packing list) and hand the goods over at our factory; the flat fee covers this preparation and handover.

Inspection and damage in transit

Please inspect the shipment on delivery. If the package is visibly damaged, do not accept it and ask the carrier to record the damage in a report. Damage in transit must be reported within 24 hours of delivery together with the carrier's damage report (see our Return & Refund Policy).
