Article 2 – Subject and applicable rules

This agreement sets out the rights and obligations of the parties for the sale and delivery of the products ordered on www.kornne.com. For buyers who are consumers, Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation apply; for commercial buyers, the Turkish Commercial Code and the Turkish Code of Obligations apply.

Article 3 – Products, prices and order summary

The products, quantities, unit prices, shipping fee, taxes and the total amount are shown on the checkout summary before the order is placed and in the order confirmation; they form an integral part of this agreement.

  • On the Turkish-language site prices are in Turkish lira (TRY) and include VAT; no separate shipping charge is added within Türkiye.
  • On all other languages prices are in US dollars (USD); Turkish VAT is not charged and a flat shipping fee of USD 100 per order is added. Import duties, customs fees and taxes of the destination country are paid by the buyer.

Article 4 – Payment

Orders can be paid by the payment methods offered at checkout:

  • Credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Troy), processed on the secure payment page of our payment service provider iyzico with 3-D Secure where required. Card details are not received or stored by the Seller.
  • Bank transfer (EFT / wire) to the Seller's accounts listed on the Bank Accounts page, quoting the order number. Orders not paid by bank transfer within 7 days may be cancelled.

An order is processed once payment has been collected: when the card payment is approved or the transfer is credited to the Seller's account. Any interest or instalment terms of a card payment are governed by the agreement between the buyer and the card-issuing bank.

Article 5 – Delivery

Products are delivered to the delivery address given by the buyer. Within Türkiye, delivery takes place within 30 days of the order confirmation at the latest. For made-to-order equipment the lead time stated on the product page or in the order confirmation applies. For deliveries outside Türkiye the dispatch date is confirmed in writing; transit and customs clearance times depend on the destination and the carrier. Details are set out in our Shipping Policy.

The buyer should inspect the shipment on delivery. Visibly damaged packages should not be accepted and the damage should be recorded in a report by the carrier. Damage in transit must be reported within 24 hours of delivery together with the carrier's report.

If delivery becomes impossible, the buyer is informed and any amount paid is refunded within 14 days.

Article 6 – Right of withdrawal (consumers)

A buyer who is a consumer may withdraw from this agreement within 14 days of delivery without giving any reason and without penalty, by sending a clear statement to info@kornne.com. The goods must be returned within 10 days of the withdrawal notice, with the invoice, complete and undamaged. The Seller refunds the amount paid within 14 days of receiving the withdrawal notice, using the same payment method (card payments are refunded to the same card). Return shipping costs are borne as described in our Return & Refund Policy, within the limits of the Distance Contracts Regulation.

The right of withdrawal does not apply to goods made to the buyer's specifications or clearly personalised, including made-to-order equipment, or to goods that have been installed or mixed with other goods after delivery.

Commercial buyers have no statutory right of withdrawal; returns are accepted under the conditions of our Return & Refund Policy.

Article 7 – Warranty

Products used and maintained according to the instructions are guaranteed against manufacturing defects for 2 years from delivery. The warranty does not cover misuse, modification or normal wear. For commercial buyers the provisions of the Turkish Commercial Code apply.

Article 8 – Obligations of the parties and force majeure

The Seller delivers the products as ordered and as described on the website. The buyer pays the order total and provides accurate information. If the information given is incorrect or the buyer cannot be reached for 15 days about a problem with the order, the Seller may cancel the order and refund any amount paid.

Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its control (natural disasters, war, strikes, changes in legislation, major breakdowns of production or communication facilities, etc.). If such an event lasts longer than 30 days, either party may terminate the agreement; amounts already paid are refunded within 14 days.

Article 9 – Personal data

Personal data given for the order is processed as described in our Privacy Policy and is shared only with the parties needed to fulfil the order (carrier, payment service provider, banks) and with authorities where required by law.

Article 10 – Disputes

Turkish law applies, without prejudice to the mandatory consumer protection rules of the buyer's country of residence. Consumers may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committees up to the amounts announced each year by the Turkish Ministry of Trade, and to the Consumer Courts at their own or the Seller's place of residence. For commercial buyers, the courts and enforcement offices at the Seller's registered seat have jurisdiction.

Article 11 – Entry into force

This agreement enters into force when the buyer accepts it electronically and places the order. The order date is the date of the agreement.
