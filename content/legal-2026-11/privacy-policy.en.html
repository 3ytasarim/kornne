This policy explains how Mezbaha Teknolojileri (“KORNNE”, “we”), the operator of www.kornne.com, processes personal data. We process personal data in line with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

Mezbaha Teknolojileri, S.S. İstanbul Mermerciler Sanayi Sitesi 22. Sokak No: 15, Köseler Mahallesi, Dilovası / Kocaeli, Türkiye. Contact: info@kornne.com · +90 535 406 80 02

Data we collect

  • Orders: company name, tax / VAT ID, contact person, email address, phone number, billing and delivery address, order contents and payment status.
  • Enquiries: the information you send us by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • Technical data: IP address (stored only in shortened / hashed form for security and abuse prevention), browser type and pages visited.
  • Analytics cookies: only if you accept them in the cookie banner; you can change your choice at any time from the link in the footer.

Card payments

Card payments are processed by our payment service provider iyzico on its secure payment page. Your card details are entered directly on that page and transmitted encrypted (SSL/TLS); we never receive or store your full card number, expiry date or security code. We only receive the payment result and a masked card reference needed for refunds and accounting.

Why we use it

  • to process and deliver your order, take payment, issue invoices and handle returns (performance of a contract);
  • to answer your enquiries and quote requests;
  • to meet legal obligations such as tax and commercial record keeping;
  • to keep the website secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interest);
  • to measure and improve the website, only with your consent.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as needed with carriers and freight forwarders (delivery), our payment service provider iyzico and banks (payment), our hosting and email providers, and public authorities where required by law. Some of these providers may process data outside Türkiye or your country with appropriate safeguards.

Security

The whole website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to order data is limited to authorised staff, and access is logged.

Retention

Order and invoice records are kept for the periods required by Turkish tax and commercial law. Enquiries are kept as long as needed to answer them and for related follow-up. Analytics data is kept according to the analytics provider's retention settings.

Your rights

You may ask us whether we process your personal data, request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or withdraw your consent at any time. Send your request to info@kornne.com; we answer within 30 days. Buyers in Türkiye can also read our KVKK information notice (Personal Data Protection) linked in the footer.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published on this page.
